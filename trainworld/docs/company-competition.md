# Company competition

Competitive rooms start from an empty world with $10 billion per company. Up to five companies operate independent networks on the same map and demand. The finance tab ranks cash and cumulative operating profit (fares minus running and maintenance costs). There is no time limit or final winner.

The server associates a browser's saved company key with its company. A different browser creates another company; losing browser storage loses the key. All companies remain reserved until the room expires, even when disconnected. Rooms expire seven days after the last join. Host handover and room checkpoints preserve ownership and accounts.

Construction quotes include only the company's enabled blueprint and additional vehicles after its own spare fleet. Construct all leaves disabled pieces and other companies' assets unchanged. Companies can inspect opponents' networks but cannot edit or connect to them. Global undo, redo, borrowing, fares, line groups, ferries and station building outlines are unavailable in competition. Cooperative rooms retain their existing controls.

Downloaded world files contain the railway map for solo play. They do not back up company keys, ownership or competitive accounts. Resume company competition through the room code in the same browser.

Competition remains a draft. Complex native edits that split or sweep nearby stations need additional adversarial coverage before release. Larger networks also need event-size and runtime-budget measurements. The host browser runs the simulation and is trusted to report results; this is not a server-authoritative anti-cheat system.
